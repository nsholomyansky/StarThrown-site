The fight to take back Earth

Humanity was cast out into the stars by AI and left to survive in Stardoms scattered across the void. Now the fight begins to take back Earth.

  1. Build your Stardom. Grow your fleet, improve your technology, and capture asteroids for resources.
  2. Join an alliance. Team up with real players. Send fleets to defend each other, coordinate in alliance chat, and research alliance technology that helps all alliance members.
  3. Take back what’s yours. Win outposts from the machines and rival alliances, and fight together for Earth at the center of the galaxy.
  4. Win the round. When the round ends, the highest-scoring Stardom and the strongest alliance top the final standings.

How it plays

Scan. Calculate. Strike.

Scan a rival, then run the Battle Calculator to see the projected result and your score change before you launch. You can scan again to see if defense has been sent, and recall your fleet before it lands. After the battle, read the battle report.

Bigger targets score more

Beat a bigger player and you score more. Beat a much smaller one and you score far less. In Star Thrown, taking on someone your own size or punching up pays more than bullying.

Attack one of us, face all of us

When a rival attacks someone in your alliance, your alliance chat gets an alert and your alliance’s Fleet Movements screen shows the incoming hostiles, where you can coordinate and calculate the defense.

A new Stardom every round

Star Thrown™ is played in rounds. When one ends, a new round begins: a new Stardom, a new strategy, and changes that may include new ship types, rebalanced stats and new features.
