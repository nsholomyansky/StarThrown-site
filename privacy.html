For in-game chat, the section below is the more specific one.

Every third party that receives player data from Star Thrown, including Google (Firebase), Apple, Brevo and Unity (Vivox), is required to protect it to the same or an equal standard as this policy.

In-game chat (Vivox)

In-game text chat is provided by Vivox, a service of Unity Technologies. Unity processes chat data on our behalf, and may also process some of it for its own purposes as described in Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Data
Your player account ID, display name, the text of the chat and direct messages you send, a device identifier and a truncated IP address.
Purpose
Delivering in-game chat between players.
Retention
Message text is deleted automatically after 7 days, and identifiers within 30 days, including after you delete your Star Thrown account.
Transfers
Processed in the United States under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; contact us for a copy.
