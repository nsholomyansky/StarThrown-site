Star Thrown Privacy Policy

Boundless Alchemy LLC
Last updated: 3 November 2026
Contact: support@starthrown.com

This privacy policy has two parts: the sections below, and our full privacy policy at https://www.iubenda.com/privacy-policy/51182608. Together they are one policy.

For in-game chat and the beta, the sections below are the more specific ones.

Every service provider that processes player data for us, including Google (Firebase), Brevo and Unity (Vivox), is required to protect it to the same or an equal standard as this policy. Services that act on their own account, such as Apple and Google for app-store payments and beta testing, Google reCAPTCHA, Google Groups, Discord and Meta (for our sign-up form on Instagram and Facebook), apply their own privacy policies.

In-game chat (Vivox)

In-game text chat is provided by Vivox, a service of Unity Technologies. Unity processes the chat data listed below only on our behalf and on our instructions, as our service provider. Separately, Unity may process technical and performance data about how the chat service runs, which does not include your messages or your account details, under its own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Data
Your player account ID, display name, the text of the chat and direct messages you send, a device identifier and a truncated IP address.
Purpose
Delivering in-game chat between players.
Retention
Message text is deleted automatically after 7 days, and identifiers within 30 days, including after you delete your Star Thrown account.
Transfers
Processed in the United States under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses; contact us for a copy.

Other data we collect

Profile pictures
If you upload a profile picture, we store it with Google (Firebase Cloud Storage). Smaller copies are shown to other players and can be viewed by anyone who has their link. Your pictures are deleted when your account is deleted.
Purchase records
Apple or Google handles every payment, and we never see your card details. For each purchase we keep the item, the store, the time and a fingerprint of the store receipt, linked to your account ID. We use them to deliver your items, handle refunds, prevent fraud and meet tax obligations. They are kept after you delete your account, and deleted automatically 7 years after the purchase or refund.
Chat diagnostics
Vivox also collects anonymous performance and diagnostic data about the chat connection, to measure and improve chat. It is not linked to your account.
Device integrity check
To protect the game from modified apps and automated abuse, the app asks Google (Firebase App Check) to confirm it is a genuine copy on a genuine device. It uses Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iOS.
Age
Star Thrown is for players aged 18 and over, as our Terms of Service require. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact support@starthrown.com.

Beta testers

This section covers the Star Thrown beta: applying at starthrown.com/beta or through our sign-up form on Instagram or Facebook, and taking part. Boundless Alchemy LLC is responsible for this data. If you live in Korea and applied through our Korean sign-up form, see For users in Korea below.

Data
When you apply: your email address, your device (iOS or Android) and, if you answer it, whether you play strategy games and, if you came through one of our ads, which post or ad it was (for example, instagram / paid). If you sign up through our form on Instagram or Facebook: your email address, your phone (iPhone or Android) and that you signed up through that form; the form cannot be sent without confirming you are 18 or older. Brevo also records when you sent the form and your IP address. If you take part: your beta sign-in account (email address and password), your username and profile picture, your game data, chat, records of any test purchases, any reports, messages you send us from the in-game Help screen (which include your account ID, stardom ID and display name) or by email, and your Discord username and what you post in our Discord server.
Purpose
Running the beta: checking that the form is not sent by a bot, sending you install links, beta updates and an end-of-beta email, running the beta game, and finding and fixing problems before launch. We also count sign-ups by the post or ad they came from, to see which work. We do not send you marketing emails. If you sign up through our form on Instagram or Facebook, Meta collects your answers there and passes them to us; we send nothing back to Meta, and we do not otherwise share beta data with advertisers or ad networks.
Legal basis
Your consent, which you give by sending the application form on starthrown.com/beta or our form on Instagram or Facebook. You can withdraw it at any time; see "Leaving the beta early" below. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.
Who receives it
  • Meta runs our sign-up form on Instagram and Facebook and sends your answers to Brevo for us. Meta also uses form data for its own purposes, such as filling in forms for you later, under Meta's Privacy Policy; Meta, not us, is responsible for that use.
  • Brevo (Brevo Inc., United States) stores your application and sends our beta emails.
  • Google reCAPTCHA checks that the form is not sent by a bot. Google's privacy policy and terms, linked under the form, apply to it.
  • On iPhone, you test through Apple TestFlight, and Apple holds your Apple Account. Apple collects crash logs and usage information from the beta app and shares them with us, and you cannot opt out. They may include your name and email address. Feedback you send through TestFlight goes to Apple and to us.
  • On Android, you join our Google Group and test through Google Play. As the group's owner, we can see your Google Account email address. Google Play shares device information and usage data from the beta app with us.
  • The beta game runs on a separate beta server with Google (Firebase), which holds your beta account and game data. In-game chat works as described in "In-game chat (Vivox)" above.
  • The beta app sends crash reports to Google (Firebase Crashlytics) and usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Fastmail hosts our support mailbox, support@starthrown.com.
  • Discord runs the server where we post beta announcements and you can report bugs. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your Discord account.
Transfers
We are based in the United States. The beta server (Google Firebase sign-in, database, profile pictures and backups) is in the United States. Apple processes TestFlight data, and Discord its data, in the United States; Fastmail processes our support mailbox in the United States and other countries; Unity processes chat data as described above. Google and Discord rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and Apple and Fastmail on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Brevo stores your application on its servers in France and Belgium; Brevo group companies in Germany, the United States and India can access it for support and maintenance, under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Standard Contractual Clauses. Meta's Privacy Policy explains where Meta processes form data.
Retention
The beta closes when the beta round ends or Star Thrown launches, whichever comes first. Within 30 days after the close, we delete the beta data we hold: your application and answers at Brevo, our Google Group, our TestFlight testers and builds, your beta account, game data, chat, test purchase records, reports and support messages, our beta Discord channels, the feedback log and tester list we keep during the beta, and the beta's Google Analytics data. Unlike the live game, we do not keep beta purchase records for 7 years or beta reports for 2 years. Applications that reach us after the close are deleted within 14 days of arriving. Some copies last longer:
  • Our backups of the beta server, and deleted profile pictures, are erased within a further 40 days.
  • Our beta server's logs are deleted automatically 30 days after they are written.
  • Google Analytics erases the beta's data permanently 35 days after we delete it.
  • Google removes deleted sign-in accounts from its systems and backups within 180 days.
  • Google (Firebase Crashlytics) keeps crash reports for 90 days, then starts deleting them.
  • In-game chat is deleted as described in "In-game chat (Vivox)" above.
  • Apple keeps TestFlight feedback for one year, and may keep crash logs and usage data until the bugs are resolved.
  • Google Play keeps feedback you send through it under Google's own policies.
  • Discord keeps its own copies under its privacy policy.
  • Brevo deletes its logs of the emails we send you one month after they are sent. It keeps its log of your form submission, including your IP address, with no time limit by default; unless you are on our launch list, we ask Brevo to delete it when the beta closes. Brevo's own backups are kept for a period it does not publish.
  • If you signed up on Instagram or Facebook, your form stays available to us in Meta for 90 days from when you sent it, then expires. We do not keep downloaded copies of it. Meta keeps its own copy under its Privacy Policy.
Launch emails
Applying for the beta does not sign you up for launch emails. You can sign up on our homepage.
Leaving the beta early
Unsubscribing from our beta emails withdraws your application: within 14 days we delete it at Brevo (or, if you are on our launch list, your beta answers). It does not delete your beta account. To delete your beta account, use Delete Account in the beta app's Account Settings, or our account deletion page. It is deleted after 7 days, as that page describes; the purchase, report and deletion records that page says we keep are deleted with the rest of the beta data, within 30 days after the beta closes; chat, crash reports and server logs follow the periods under Retention above. To leave our Google Group or the beta altogether, email support@starthrown.com, and we remove your application and your Group membership within 14 days.
Your rights
Your rights over your data, including the right to complain to a data protection authority, are set out in our full privacy policy at https://www.iubenda.com/privacy-policy/51182608. If you are in Brazil, you also have the rights in Article 18 of the LGPD, including the right to petition the ANPD.

For users in Korea · 한국 이용자 안내

이 항목은 대한민국에 계신 분이 Instagram의 한국어 신청 양식으로 Star Thrown™ 베타를 신청하는 경우의 개인정보 처리방침입니다(「개인정보 보호법」 제30조). 개인정보처리자는 Boundless Alchemy LLC(미국, 이하 '당사')입니다. 이 항목에 없는 사항은 위 'Beta testers' 항목(영문)을 따릅니다.

처리하는 개인정보 항목
이메일 주소, 플레이할 휴대폰 종류(iPhone/Android), 이 양식(광고)을 통해 신청했다는 사실, 신청 일시. 신청 과정에서 IP 주소가 자동으로 기록될 수 있습니다.
처리 목적
베타 설치 링크 이메일과 베타 운영에 필요한 안내(일정 변경, 베타 종료 등) 발송, iPhone/Android별 신청자 수 파악과 기기별 문제 안내, 어느 광고를 통해 신청하셨는지 집계. 정식 출시 소식 등 광고성 정보는 보내지 않습니다.
신청 연령
만 19세 이상만 신청할 수 있습니다. 당사는 만 14세 미만 아동의 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다.
처리 및 보유 기간
베타 종료(베타 라운드 종료일과 정식 출시일 중 빠른 날) 후 지체 없이, 늦어도 30일 이내에 삭제합니다. 베타 종료 후 접수된 신청은 지체 없이 삭제합니다. 수신을 거부하시거나 동의를 철회하시면 지체 없이(늦어도 10일 이내) 삭제합니다. 이메일 발송 기록은 발송 1개월 후 삭제됩니다. 당사가 Meta에서 신청서를 열람할 수 있는 기간은 제출일로부터 90일입니다.
파기 절차 및 방법
보유 기간이 끝나거나 삭제를 요청받으면 Brevo에서 해당 연락처와 신청 정보를 삭제하고, Brevo가 남긴 신청 기록(IP 주소 등)은 베타 종료 시 Brevo에 삭제를 요청합니다. 당사는 내려받은 사본을 보관하지 않습니다. Brevo는 매일 백업하며 삭제된 정보는 복구할 수 없다고 밝히고 있습니다(백업 보관 기간은 Brevo가 공개하지 않음). 법령에 따라 따로 보존하는 정보는 없습니다.
제3자 제공
당사는 신청 정보를 제3자에게 제공하지 않으며, Apple이나 Google에도 제공하지 않습니다. 다만 Meta는 Meta 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 이 양식의 정보를 직접 수집·이용하는 별도의 개인정보처리자이며(예: 이후 양식 자동 입력), 그 이용과 보관은 Meta가 책임집니다. TestFlight, Google 그룹, Google Play에는 신청자가 직접 가입·설치하며, 이때 각 회사가 자체 방침에 따라 정보를 수집합니다.
처리 위탁 및 국외 이전

당사는 아래 두 회사에 처리를 위탁하며, 이에 따라 위 항목 전체가 국외로 이전됩니다. 이전 근거는 신청 양식에서 따로 받는 국외 이전 동의(「개인정보 보호법」 제28조의8 제1항 제1호)입니다. 당사는 미국에서 개인정보를 처리합니다.

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.(미국, 1 Meta Way, Menlo Park, CA 94025, ATTN: Privacy Operations, 연락처: Meta 개인정보 처리방침). 위탁 업무: 신청 양식 운영과 제출된 답변의 Brevo 자동 전송. 이전 시기와 방법: 양식을 제출할 때 인터넷으로. 처리 국가: 미국, 아일랜드, 덴마크, 스웨덴 등 Meta가 인프라·데이터센터를 둔 곳, Meta 제품이 제공되는 국가, Meta의 파트너·서비스 제공업체가 있는 국가. 보유 기간: 당사가 열람할 수 있는 기간은 제출일로부터 90일이며, Meta 자체 이용분은 Meta 방침을 따릅니다.
  • Brevo Inc.(미국, 823 Congress Ave. Ste 300, Austin, TX 78701, 연락처: Brevo 개인정보 처리방침). 위탁 업무: 신청 정보 저장과 베타 이메일 발송. 이전 시기와 방법: 제출 후 Meta와 Brevo의 연동을 통해 인터넷으로 자동 전송. 저장 위치: Brevo 그룹(모회사 Brevo SAS, 프랑스)의 프랑스·벨기에 서버이며, 유지보수와 지원을 위해 Brevo 그룹사(미국·인도·독일)가 접근할 수 있습니다. 보유 기간: 위 '처리 및 보유 기간'과 같습니다.

국외 이전에 동의하지 않으시려면 양식을 제출하지 않으시면 됩니다. 이 경우 신청이 접수되지 않아 베타 설치 링크를 받으실 수 없습니다.

정보주체의 권리와 행사 방법
언제든 개인정보 열람·정정·삭제·처리정지를 요구하고 동의를 철회하실 수 있습니다. 보내 드리는 이메일 하단의 '수신 거부' 링크를 누르거나 support@starthrown.com으로 연락해 주세요. 동의를 철회하시면 지체 없이(늦어도 10일 이내) 삭제하며, 열람·정정·삭제·처리정지 요구에는 요구를 받은 날부터 10일 이내에 조치하고 결과를 알려 드립니다. 요구에 따를 수 없는 경우에는 그 이유와 이의 제기 방법을 함께 알려 드립니다.
안전성 확보 조치
신청 정보는 당사 대표자만 접근할 수 있는 Brevo 계정에 보관하고, 신청서 사본(CSV 등)을 내려받아 보관하지 않습니다. Meta와 Brevo는 각자의 보안 조치를 적용합니다.
자동 수집 장치
신청 양식은 Meta가 운영하며, 당사는 이 양식과 관련해 쿠키 등 자동 수집 장치를 설치·운영하지 않습니다. Meta의 쿠키는 Meta 방침을 따릅니다.
